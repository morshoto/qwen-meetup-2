The [allocation proposal](../research/rsi/qwen38-weight-allocation-proposal.json) is **not an execution registration**. Its self-digest is `581d0250bf5aa1b9874094e12b16b151fdb9ea5dad8f9ef406279ab2b3f669de`. It was generated before any new ARC generation or Gen 1 evaluation. The unreviewed lineage input is empty; neither that fact nor the checks below establish semantic independence or complete historical and model-pretraining non-exposure. Review the historical and lineage inventory and freeze a separate execution registration before collection.

The proposal uses seed 20261111, reserves 25 validation parents and selects 40 fresh parents, with a 160-parent shortfall against the requested maximum of 200. It conservatively excludes the previously scored 200, the 100 proposed Phase B training IDs, prior protected 200/50 IDs, known exposures, earlier development/selection tasks, the existing local comparison 50, the 20 scheduled strict-RSI generation slots, and the proposed 50-task Phase B comparison set. The latter is **not frozen**; it was read from PR #10 head `4d6a9bc21e8756ce375ce493675bca71e49af221` and conservatively retained to avoid a conflicting proposal. The union contains 934 Training IDs. Official 120 evaluation challenges are separately excluded by ID and matching visible input; official evaluation solutions were not read.

An independent read-only check recomputed the proposal digest, found zero selected ID or exact input-grid overlaps with the exclusion union or official challenges, and found no shared input grids among the 65 selected parents. It checked all 65 selected Training solution fingerprints without publishing their grids. The Training challenges and solutions match the local competition archive byte for byte. This check does not prove semantic independence, discover all derived variants, or authenticate an unrecorded lineage. The archive, reservation source, model, prompt and source-code fingerprints are in the proposal. A source change requires a new proposal and audit; this proposal does not authorize the older collection path.

A subsequent **provisional offline** FP8 re-verification used the proposal's exclusion union except the prior Phase A set itself (the historical source), plus both proposed fresh and validation parents. The 16 are a **provisional audit yield**, not a training dataset or a claim of current 4-bit self-training: the lineage register is still unreviewed, token lengths and the final registration are not checked, and no private training records or adapter were created.
